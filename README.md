# 🌟 Kero Travel (كيرو ترافيل) - Luxury Travel & VIP Limousine

A modern, responsive, and luxurious website for **Kero Travel**, featuring full Arabic (RTL) and English (LTR) bilingual support, a dynamic WhatsApp booking engine, interactive fleet showcase, and embedded Alexandria map.

---

## 🚗 VIP Fleet (أسطول سيارات كيرو ترافيل)

- 🚐 **Toyota HiAce VIP (تويوتا هايس VIP سياحي)**: 14 Passengers, High Roof, Dual AC, Massive Luggage.
- 🚙 **Mitsubishi Xpander (ميتسوبيشي إكسباندر 7 راكب)**: 7 Passengers, High Ground Clearance, Coastal & Safari.
- 🚗 **Hyundai Elantra CN7 White (هيونداي إلنترا CN7 بيضاء)**: 4 Passengers, Airport Limousine (Borg El Arab / Cairo).
- 🚘 **Hyundai Elantra CN7 Black VIP (هيونداي إلنترا CN7 سوداء)**: 4 Passengers, Executive VIP, Business Class.

- **📍 Location Integration:** Embedded Google Map of Alexandria Governorate, Egypt.
